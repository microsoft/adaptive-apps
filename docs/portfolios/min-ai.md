# The Min-AI Portfolio

The Min-AI Portfolio extends the [Min Portfolio](min.md) with capability to deploy and manage local AI models, such as Small Language Models (SLM) for continuous offline inferences when disconnected from cloud. This portfolio enables:

* Continued AI inferences when disconnected from the internet, enabled by local AI models.

## Components

* [Min Portfolio Components](./min.md#components)
* +[Kaito](https://kaito-project.github.io/kaito/docs/) for offline AI model management on Kubernetes
